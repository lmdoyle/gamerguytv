Star Wars Battlefront II — current loadout & playstyle

Star Wars Battlefront II is one of my all-time favourite games to stream. I main the Officer class for its squad support and versatility — the ability to drop a battle command, set up a turret, and keep the squad alive while racking up eliminations is hard to beat.

On the hero side, I main Leia Organa on the light side and Bossk on the dark side. Leia's Squad Shield and crowd control keep the team alive in chaotic fights, while Bossk's zone denial and hunter instincts make him a constant threat up close.

Whether it's Galactic Assault, Supremacy, or Heroes vs. Villains, you'll usually find me somewhere in the mix causing chaos. Follow along on Twitch to catch live sessions or check out YouTube for highlights.

Game Mode Strategy

Galactic Assault & Supremacy — In the large-scale modes I almost always run Officer. The key is positioning yourself near objectives rather than chasing kills. Drop your turret at a chokepoint, use Battle Command on teammates pushing the objective, and keep your squad alive with Officer's Presence. You won't always top the scoreboard but you'll consistently be one of the highest-impact players in the match.

Heroes vs. Villains — This is where I spend the most time. The mode is a 4v4 format where each team has one hero or villain and the rest play as elite troopers. Communication and target prioritisation matter more than individual skill — focusing the same target as your team nearly always beats going solo. When playing as Luke, I use Rush aggressively to close gaps and isolate targets, then Repulse when troopers mob me. As Boba Fett, staying airborne and forcing enemies to look up while your team pressures from the ground is the most reliable strategy.

General Tips — Star Cards make a significant difference at higher levels of play. For Luke, I run Intensified Fight Back (increased damage reduction during Rush), Epicenter (wider Repulse range), and Saber Throw Range. For Boba, Bounty Hunter's Instincts, Rocket Barrage, and Flames of the Underworld are the core loadout. Don't neglect Star Cards early — they're the biggest performance gap between new and experienced players.
