Stream Schedule

When to catch GamerGuyTV live

Never Miss a Stream

The best way to know when I'm live is to follow and turn on notifications on Twitch. You'll get a notification the moment I go live — no need to check back manually.

Follow on Twitch Subscribe on YouTube

What Each Stream Looks Like

Battlefront Sessions
Star Wars Battlefront II
Competitive multiplayer across Heroes vs. Villains, Galactic Assault, and Supremacy. These streams tend to be high-energy with lots of moment-to-moment action. Good for viewers who enjoy watching skilled play and tactical conversation about loadouts and strategy.
Nintendo Switch Sessions
Switch Variety
Mario Kart, Smash Bros, and whatever else I'm working through at the time. These are more casual and conversational — great for viewer interaction, chat voting on game choices, and a generally relaxed atmosphere.
Dev Streams
Unity & Android Development
Occasional streams where I work on an app or game project live. These are slower-paced and more educational — good for viewers interested in game development, Android apps, or just seeing what the creation process actually looks like in real time.
New Game Sessions
First Playthroughs
When something new catches my attention I'll do a first-playthrough stream. No prior knowledge, genuine reactions, and open chat discussion about the game as we go. These are some of my favourite streams to run because the unpredictability keeps things interesting.

Missed a Stream?

Past broadcast VODs are available on Twitch for a limited time after each stream. For longer-term access, highlights and full session uploads go to YouTube — subscribing there is the best way to catch content you missed.

Short clips and highlights also go up on TikTok regularly. If you just want the best moments without watching a full session, TikTok is the place to check.

Community

The stream community is one of the parts of content creation I value most. I genuinely try to respond to chat and remember returning viewers — it doesn't feel like a stream when it's just shouting into an empty room, and I appreciate everyone who shows up and participates.

The community is welcoming to new viewers. Whether you've been following since the early days or you just found the channel today, the atmosphere is the same: relaxed, respectful, and focused on having a good time. No toxicity, no aggressive moderation for normal behaviour — just people watching games together.

If you have questions about the channel, games I play, or anything else, the easiest ways to reach me are through Twitch chat during a live stream or via the social links on the About page.
